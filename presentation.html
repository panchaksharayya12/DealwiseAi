01 • Market Need

The Real Estate Buying Problem

The Price Illusion

Buyers evaluate properties by location and square footage alone, failing to calculate capitalization yields, maintenance drag, and actual net cashflow.

Negative Carry Shock

High LTV loans paired with interest rates and maintenance fees often cause unanticipated monthly deficits where out-of-pocket costs exceed rental income.

AI Financial Hallucination

Standard LLMs invent financial figures, miscalculate compound amortization, and hallucinate rates, creating dangerous investment guidance.

Broker-Biased Portals

Traditional real-estate listing websites earn broker commissions. Their UX is optimized to sell properties, not to uncover hidden deal risks.

03 • Financial Engine

Actuarial Mathematics & Yields

Core Financial Formulas

  • Gross Yield: (Annual Rent / Asking Price) × 100
  • Net Yield: ((Rent - Maintenance) × 12 / Asking Price) × 100
  • Price / sq.ft: Asking Price / Built-up Area
  • Cash-on-Cash ROI: (Net Annual Cashflow / Down Payment) × 100

Loan Amortization Engine

  • Standard Reducing EMI Formula: P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)
  • Total Interest Computed: (EMI × n) - Loan Amount
  • LTV Ratio: (Loan Amount / Asking Price) × 100
  • Monthly Carry: Rent - Maintenance - Monthly EMI
04 • Scoring Methodology

The 5-Pillar Deal Score

Transparent Weighted Weights

  • Financial Health (35%): Net yield benchmark, cashflow coverage.
  • Rental Yield Power (20%): Metropolitan yield comparison.
  • Price Efficiency (20%): Price per sq.ft affordability.
  • Loan & Debt Burden (15%): LTV ratio and debt service.
  • Risk Buffer (10%): Asset age, maintenance drag, parking.

Score Classifications

  • 80 – 100: Strong Deal (Healthy yield, positive carry, low debt risk)
  • 60 – 79: Fair Deal (Viable deal, reasonable yields, minor caveats)
  • 40 – 59: Needs Review (Yield compression, debt burden present)
  • 0 – 39: Risky Deal (Severe negative carry, high leverage danger)
